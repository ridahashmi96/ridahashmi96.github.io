❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Will snap make me look like I'm still in the meeting?

No. snap completely disconnects you from the call. From the other participants' perspective, you simply leave the meeting, which is quite standard.

Do I need any technical skills to use this?

Not at all. If you can use a web browser and click a button, you can use snap. It is designed to be as user-friendly as possible.

Is snap safe to use?

Yes. snap is a straightforward utility that only controls your connection to Google Meet for you. It does not modify your system or interact with other apps. It simply automates the "leave meeting" action to save you time.

Will this work on any computer?

As long as you can run a standard web browser and our downloaded app, you should be fine. It is built to be lightweight and run on most modern Windows computers.

Can I use snap when I'm not in a meeting?

Technically yes, but it's designed for use during a Google Meet call. When you click it while in a meeting, it will disconnect you. If you open it when you're not in a call, it will just sit quietly in your taskbar.
